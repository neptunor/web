The Kayak Hybrid 335 bridges the gap between drop-stitch SUP rigidity and traditional kayak comfort. Thick PVC/Hypalon side tubes create a protective recessed cockpit that deflects side splashes while boosting secondary stability and anti-capsizing capability.

Symmetrical V-shaped bow and stern cones slice through water efficiently, reducing drag and improving straight-line tracking. A triangular splash deck at the bow features a criss-cross bungee cargo net for securing dry bags, life vests and essential gear.

High-visibility Teal/Turquoise primary color contrasts with a charcoal gray drop-stitch floor and black anti-abrasion edge reinforcements, delivering strong visual depth and safety visibility. Polynesian-inspired graphic accents on the forward tube sections add a vibrant, sporty character.

The modular drop-stitch floor — using the same high-pressure technology as SUP boards — provides a rigid, flat standing deck. Designed to be removable, it simplifies post-use cleaning, drying and compact folding. Heavy-duty reinforced webbed handles at bow, stern and sides facilitate multi-person carrying.

The Alloy 360 is the entry point into Neptunor's welded aluminum RIB range. At 3.6 m LOA, it combines a lightweight marine-alloy hull with a three-chamber inflatable tube set that provides excellent stability at rest and a soft, dry ride in choppy conditions.

Built on a press-formed and CNC-trimmed alloy jig, every hull comes off the line with identical geometry — critical for fleet programs where consoles, graphics and hardware must align across dozens of units. The self-draining deck channels water aft through scuppers, eliminating the standing-water problems that plague cheaper inflatable platforms.

The 360 is the default starting point for yacht tender programs (its 0.42 m tube diameter fits most davit cradles) and coastal rental fleets (where its light displacement makes beach launches trivial). The standard configuration seats four on jockey-style bolsters with a center console, but the platform accepts center-console, side-console or tiller configurations depending on your market.

Tube fabric options include 1100 dtex PVC (standard) and CSM/Hypalon (premium), both with heat-welded seams rated at 0.35 bar for a 24-hour pressure-hold test. Every tube set is pressure-tested before it leaves the factory; any chamber that drops more than 0.02 bar in 24 hours is rejected.

The alloy hull is finished in two-part marine primer ready for your gelcoat or paint scheme. Optional extras include bow locker, transom steps, under-deck fuel tank, navigation light kit and custom graphics from a single-color logo to full-wrap livery.

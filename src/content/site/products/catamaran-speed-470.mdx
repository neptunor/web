The Catamaran Speed 470 is Neptunor's high-performance inflatable catamaran, built for racing, surf rescue and fast patrol missions. The twin-hull architecture eliminates the need for a traditional keel — the two outer sponsons act as dual stabilizers, delivering razor-sharp tracking and zero body roll at speed.

A high-pressure drop-stitch air floor inflates to rock-hard rigidity, providing a stable standing platform for anglers and rescue crews while keeping overall weight low enough for single-person deployment. The open-bow design allows seamless beach landings and rapid water extraction.

Tube fabric options include 1100 dtex PVC (standard) and CSM/Hypalon (premium), both with heat-welded seams rated at 0.35 bar for a 24-hour pressure-hold test. The dual-sponson configuration channels air beneath the hull, creating an aerodynamic cushion that reduces friction and maximizes fuel efficiency with smaller outboard motors.

Four styling collections are available: Solid (minimalist block-color), Two-Tone (modern sport), Racing Stripes (high-visibility geometric) and Tactical Camo (military-grade printed coating with anti-UV top coat). All colorways are custom-matchable to Pantone Coated (C) specifications.

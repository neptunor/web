The Glass 520 is the 5.2 m best-seller in the Neptunor fiberglass RIB range — a full-featured family cruiser platform that dealer partners customize more than any other model.

At 5.20 m LOA with a 2.10 m beam, the 520 offers genuine overnight capability with a full-width aft bench, bow sunpad area, and enough deck space for ten passengers. The 60 HP engine rating provides the performance needed for watersports towing while remaining manageable for recreational operators.

The double-console option creates a passenger-side forward seat with storage, while the ski-mast and tow-point package transforms the 520 into a capable watersports platform. The aft boarding ladder with fold-down step makes re-boarding after swimming effortless.

This platform is the starting point for most dealer customization programs — its clean hull lines, generous livery surfaces and mid-range price point make it the ideal canvas for branded fleet programs, charter operations and premium rental fleets.

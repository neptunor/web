The Airdock 330 is Neptunor's purpose-built rental dinghy — a 3.3 m air-deck inflatable designed for the daily abuse of high-rotation rental operations at coastal resorts, marinas and activity centers.

The reinforced air-deck floor uses a heavier drop-stitch specification than leisure models, standing up to sand, gravel and concrete beach launches that destroy cheaper floors within a season. Multi-position grab lines along both gunwales give passengers secure handholds during boarding, transit and recovery.

The hull shape is optimized for beach launch and recovery — a shallow-V entry that slides onto sand easily, with a pronounced keel line that tracks straight in following seas. The self-draining deck channels water aft, keeping the interior dry during rental use.

UV-stable tube fabric resists degradation from constant sun exposure, extending service life in tropical rental environments. Every Airdock 330 is pressure-tested to 0.35 bar and delivered with a numbered build plate for fleet inventory tracking.

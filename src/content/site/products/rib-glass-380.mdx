The Glass 380 is Neptunor's entry-level fiberglass RIB, designed as a proven dealer platform that combines a deep-V GRP hull with an inflatable collar for the best of both worlds — soft ride quality and stable mooring characteristics.

At 3.8 m LOA, the 380 is trailer-friendly and easily solo-launched, making it ideal for retail dealers who need a boat that sells itself on the showroom floor and performs reliably in customer hands. The automotive-grade gelcoat finish accepts PANTONE color matching, enabling bright, distinctive livery that differentiates your brand from competitors.

The deep-V hull section cuts through chop with minimal slamming, while the three-chamber inflatable tube provides a generous 0.43 m of buoyancy above the waterline. This combination delivers the dry, comfortable ride that recreational buyers expect at a price point that opens retail doors.

Standard configuration includes center console with steering, jockey seating for five, self-draining deck and bow locker. Optional upgrades include bow sun deck, aft bench seat, stainless steel rail package, navigation light kit and custom gelcoat colors.

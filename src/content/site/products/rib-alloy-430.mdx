The Alloy 430 is the mid-size workhorse of the Neptunor aluminum RIB range. At 4.3 m LOA with a deeper-V hull section than its 360 sibling, it delivers a drier, more composed ride in the 0.5–1.0 m chop that typically grounds lighter inflatables.

The four-chamber tube set provides a higher freeboard and greater payload capacity — enough to carry six adults plus dive gear, fishing equipment or patrol supplies. The reinforced transom is rated for outboards up to 40 HP, giving the 430 the speed and reserve power needed for commercial patrol, resort transfer and dive-support operations.

Standard features include lifting davit points rated for overhead crane recovery (essential for yacht tender programs), a self-draining deck with oversized scuppers, and a gunwale track system for mounting accessories without drilling the hull.

The 430 is the most popular platform for Mediterranean rental fleets, where its combination of durability, passenger capacity and towability makes it ideal for high-rotation summer operations. It is equally at home as a harbor workboat, diving support platform or resort guest-transfer vessel.

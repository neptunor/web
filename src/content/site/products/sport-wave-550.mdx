The Wave 550 stretches the sport console concept for offshore-minded buyers — a 5.5 m platform with a deeper-V hull, larger console with head compartment, aft sunpad seating and a 90 HP rating that delivers genuinely thrilling performance.

Where the compact Wave 450 targets rental operators and lifestyle brands, the 550 targets premium dealers and charter operators who need a fast, comfortable day boat that can handle offshore conditions with confidence.

The console includes a head compartment (marine toilet optional), electronics mounting surface and wrap-around windscreen. The aft sunpad converts to a casting platform or watersports staging area, and the gelcoat-and-livery color system enables coordinated brand identities across fleet programs.

The Raft White 380 is Neptunor's self-bailing inflatable whitewater raft, engineered for Class III–V river wilderness, commercial outfitter operations and premium private expeditions. The raised I-Beam inflatable floor forces water to the edges and evacuates it through perimeter bailing holes within seconds — no manual bucketing required.

The flat-bottom, zero-keel design ensures a shallow draft that glides effortlessly over hidden shoals and riverbeds, enabling instant 360-degree pivot maneuvers in tight, rock-strewn rapid channels where a V-hull would stall or flip. Heavy-duty rubbing strakes along the waterline mask muddy river stains while protecting the core tubes from side impacts.

Frame-ready modular construction allows aluminum fishing or rowing frames to lock into the rigid perimeter tubes, converting the raft into a luxury drift boat with swivel seats, anchor systems and coolers. Drop-stitch air deck upgrades (10–15 psi) provide rock-solid standing stability for fly casting.

Colorway options span Glacier White / Cement Gray hull bases with Neon Green or Neon Pink accents for commercial "Insta-ready" aesthetics, through Tactical Olive and Canyon Desert Tan for expedition stealth, to Navy Blue tone-on-tone for premium private buyers.

The Guard 470 is Neptunor's fast-response rescue platform — a 4.7 m boat configured for first-response agencies that need a reliable, deployable rescue craft with the documentation required for government procurement.

Built on the proven Alloy 470 hull, the Guard 470 adds reinforced sponsons for improved stability in side-on recovery operations, rescue-rack mounting points for stretcher and equipment transport, and a self-righting bag option that returns the boat to upright after capsize.

The casualty recovery zone occupies the aft section of the deck, providing a flat, unobstructed area for medical assessment and first aid. Grab lines run along both gunwales for swimmer and survivor recovery, and the reinforced transom accepts rescue-specific equipment mounts.

Every Guard 470 ships with technical documentation packages ready for agency procurement files — including CE certification documents, stability calculations, material test certificates and operational manuals in English and Spanish.

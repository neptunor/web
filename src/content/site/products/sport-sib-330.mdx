The Sport SIB 330 is Neptunor's classic foldable sport inflatable boat — the most mature and proven model in the international SIB market. Removable aluminum floor boards provide exceptional rigidity and load capacity, while aluminum side stringers lock the boards laterally to prevent hull flexing at high speed.

The inflatable V-keel chamber sits between the floor boards and the outer bottom fabric, shaping the hull into a V-profile that improves wave-cutting and prevents sideways skidding. Multi-chamber tube construction (3+1 or 4+1 compartments) ensures sufficient buoyancy even if one chamber is compromised.

A transom protection plate with marine plywood and aluminum motor pads shields the transom from outboard clamps. The self-bailing drain valve uses Bernoulli's principle to flush cockpit water while underway. Grab lines and molded handles provide safe re-boarding from the water.

Material options include 0.9–1.2 mm PVC fabric (hot-air/high-frequency welded) for standard use and CSM/Hypalon (cold-bonded) for premium or high-UV environments. Heavy-duty rubbing strakes and armor pads protect the keel and high-friction zones during beach landings.

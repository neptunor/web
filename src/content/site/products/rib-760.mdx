Neptunor’s largest fiberglass platform — a 7.6 m GRP hull with a 7-chamber collar, 300 L fuel capacity and up to 250 HP for serious offshore, charter and crew-transfer duty.

Standard equipment includes 2 oars; foot pump; repair kit; front and back cabin with cushions; center console with windscreen and 4 point switch and fuel gauge; seat box; bilge pump; fiberglass anchor tray; 300L built-in fuel tank; fiberglass or stainless steel targa with 3 navigation lights.

Optional equipment and OEM tailoring: 8 kgs anchor, life jacket, stainless steel T-TOP sunroof or foldable sunroof, electric pump, trailer, teak floor, boat cover, 100A battery with box, hydraulic steering system, shower system, electric anchor system, luxury anchor tray with storage.

The KaBoat Fish 375 merges the high-speed performance and exceptional stability of a catamaran with the lightweight portability of a kayak. The dual-sponson design naturally channels air under the hull, creating an aerodynamic cushion that reduces friction and delivers blistering top speeds with smaller-horsepower outboards.

Four factory-fitted multi-position rod holders provide flexible trolling angles. Dual removable aluminum bench seats enhance structural rigidity while allowing customizable seating. A bow splash guard with tension tie-downs deflects head waves and keeps the cockpit dry.

The keel-less architecture uses twin outer hulls as primary stabilizers, eliminating body roll and providing razor-sharp tracking during high-speed turns. A high-pressure drop-stitch air floor inflates to ultra-high rigidity, allowing anglers to stand, walk and cast with total confidence.

The open-bow design is purpose-engineered for fast beach landings, easy water reboarding and rapid equipment loading — widely adopted by international surf lifesaving teams and flood relief units.

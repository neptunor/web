The Airtender 270 is Neptunor's compact deck tender — a 2.7 m inflatable with a drop-stitch air deck that provides rigid-floor stability while folding into a carry bag smaller than a standard suitcase.

At 12 kg, the Airtender 270 is light enough to carry single-handed from car trunk to waterline. The drop-stitch air deck inflates to 0.8 bar, creating a rigid platform that feels solid underfoot while remaining significantly lighter than aluminum or plywood floor systems.

The fold-flat transom accommodates outboards up to 10 HP, giving the 270 enough power for short harbor transfers and tender runs. When deflated, the entire boat — tubes, floor, transom and accessories — packs into the supplied carry bag for stowage in a yacht lazarette, vehicle trunk or airline luggage compartment.

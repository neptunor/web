The Alloy 470 is Neptunor's heavy-duty aluminum platform. At 4.7 m with a thickened keel guard, five-chamber tube set and space for a full jockey console, it is configured for professional crews who prioritize payload, durability and seaworthiness over recreational frills.

The hull features a reinforced keel plate that protects against beach launches, dock impacts and shallow-water grounding — common hazards in professional operations. The five-chamber tube set provides redundant buoyancy: even with one chamber deflated, the boat remains stable and afloat.

The 470 accommodates up to eight persons with a jockey-style helm console that positions the driver low and central for maximum visibility and control in rough conditions. Optional configurations include twin console (driver + passenger), tiller steering for minimalist patrol setups, and flat-deck conversion for cargo or equipment transport.

This platform serves coast guard auxiliary units, harbor master departments, fishery inspection services and commercial dive teams across 40+ countries. Its combination of aluminum durability and inflatable-tube stability makes it the preferred choice for agencies that operate daily in demanding marine environments.

The Glass 640 is the flagship of the Neptunor fiberglass RIB range — a 6.4 m offshore-capable platform with a stepped deep-V hull, enclosed console with marine head, and the build quality to match premium European brands at a fraction of the cost.

The stepped hull design reduces wetted surface area for improved fuel efficiency and higher top speeds, while the six-chamber tube set provides exceptional stability and reserve buoyancy. The 115 HP engine rating gives the 640 genuine offshore range — capable of reaching offshore reefs, islands and anchorages that smaller platforms cannot safely access.

The enclosed console includes a marine head compartment, electronics mounting surface and weather protection for the helmsman. This configuration makes the 640 suitable for extended day trips, charter operations and coastal patrol assignments where crew comfort matters.

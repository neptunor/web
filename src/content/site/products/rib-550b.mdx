A 5.5 m fiberglass RIB that adds a console, aft sun deck, bow and stern seating, arch roll bar and a 100 L tank — 90 HP and a 5-chamber collar. The dressed-up 550 for retail and rental lines.

Standard equipment includes 2 oars; foot pump / bilge pump; repair kit / 100L built in fuel tank; center console; butterfly anchor tray; front and back cabin with cushion; stainless steel back rest; sun deck with cushion; fiberglass or stainless steel arch roll bar with 3 lights.

Optional equipment and OEM tailoring: 5.5kgs anchor, life jacket, foldable sunroof, electric pump, 60A battery with box, hydraulic steering system, trailer/ boat cover / teak floor, fiberglass step ends, stainless steel handrails / stainless steel C ladder.

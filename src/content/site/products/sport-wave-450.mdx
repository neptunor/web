The Wave 450 is Neptunor's compact sport console — a 4.5 m platform tuned for water skiing, tubing and fast coastal runs. The stepped aft deck provides a flat, stable platform for watersports launches, while the bucket-seat helm positions the driver for maximum control at speed.

Designed for rental operators and lifestyle brands that target younger demographics, the Wave 450 speaks a visual language of speed and energy. Its wraparound livery printing surface allows full-coverage brand graphics that stand out on the water and photograph well for social media.

The ski tow point is rated for single-rider and two-rider towing loads, and the aft deck's low freeboard makes re-boarding after falls quick and easy. Optional accessories include board racks, speaker pods and LED accent lighting.

The Kayak Tour 365 is a full drop-stitch inflatable kayak that eliminates the compromises of traditional round-tube designs. High-pressure drop-stitch panels form flat, rigid sidewalls that give the hull clean, crisp lines with zero inflated bulges — visually and structurally indistinguishable from a hard-shell touring kayak.

V-shaped molded bow and stern cones in hard polyurethane slice through water efficiently, protecting the hull on shallow beach and rock impacts while enhancing hydrodynamic displacement. An integrated rigid cockpit rim (coaming) emulates the classic oval opening of a traditional hard-shell touring kayak, helping block water splashes.

The high-contrast racing palette pairs a slate-gray lower hull with high-visibility Safety Orange cockpit rim and splash deck, delivering safety visibility and a sleek racing aesthetic. Geometric dot-matrix wave graphics and italicized sans-serif side branding complete the look.

A low-profile embedded bow bungee system mounts flush with the flat drop-stitch deck, eliminating bulky protrusions. High-back professional kayak seats integrate cleanly inside the deep drop-stitch side walls. Compact rubberized bow and stern handles sit flush with the nose cones for easy transport.

The Patrol 600 is Neptunor's long-range patrol platform — a 6.0 m boat built for coast guards, port authorities and security contractors who need extended range, crew endurance and equipment mounting capacity.

The jockey-style seating system provides shock absorption for high-speed patrols in rough seas, reducing crew fatigue during extended operations. The radar arch provision supports antenna arrays, searchlights and communication equipment, while the twin fuel tank option doubles the range for extended patrol assignments.

Night-operation lighting prep includes pre-wired conduits for navigation lights, searchlight power, deck floodlights and blue/red emergency lighting. The vessel is delivered ready for agency-specific lighting packages to be installed without hull modifications.

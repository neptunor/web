The Dive Team 420 is a purpose-built dive support platform — a 4.2 m boat designed for dive schools, commercial diving teams and marine research operations that need a stable, equipment-friendly work platform on the water.

The flat aft deck section provides a staging area for tank assembly, weight belt preparation and buddy checks, while integrated tank racks secure cylinders during transit. The dive-rated entry/exit ladder with handrails enables controlled water entry and exit for divers of all experience levels.

Optional upgrades include dive-flag visibility packages (flag pole mount, strobe light and surface marker buoy rack), oxy-tank mounting bracket and a GPS/chart plotter for marking dive sites and returning precisely.

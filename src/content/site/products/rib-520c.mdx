A 5.2 m fiberglass RIB that adds a cushioned sun deck and stainless fittings to the console layout — 360 kg dry, 85 HP and 70 L fuel options.

Standard equipment includes 2 oars; foot pump; front and back cabin with cushions; repair kit; center console; bilge pump; fiberglass anchor tray; fiberglass or stainless steel targa with 3 navigation lights; stainless steel handles; sun deck cushion.

Optional equipment and OEM tailoring: 5.5 kgs anchor, life jacket, aluminum sunroof, electric pump, fiberglass step ends, trailer, teak floor, boat cover, 60A battery with box, mechanical steering system, hydraulic steering system, 70L built in fuel tank.

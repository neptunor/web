Neptunor will exhibit at **Metstrade 2026** (RAI Amsterdam, 17–19 November), the marine industry's largest B2B trade exhibition. On stand, our team will present the factory's OEM/ODM boat programs — and meeting slots for private-label tenders and fleet programs are now open for booking.

## What we are bringing to Amsterdam

Metstrade is where European buying decisions for the season ahead get made, so we are arriving prepared for working sessions rather than brochure handouts:

- **Alloy Series aluminum-hull RIBs** — the rib-alloy-360, rib-alloy-430 and rib-alloy-470 platforms, with hull samples, console options and CE design-category documentation for EU-bound builds.
- **Tender line concepts** — airtender-270 and airdock-330 air-deck dinghies configured as private-label packages, including retail packaging produced in-house.
- **Material wall** — PVC and CSM (Hypalon®) tube fabric swatches with test data behind every batch: tensile, UV aging and pressure-hold records from our own lab.
- **Fleet program desk** — spares-kit planning, livery matching and multi-site rollout schedules for marina groups, rental operators and rescue organizations.

## Who should book a meeting

We are scheduling 30-minute working meetings throughout the three days, prioritized for:

1. **Brands and distributors** planning private-label or co-branded boat ranges for the 2027 season.
2. **Marina, resort and rental groups** standardizing tender and activity fleets across multiple sites.
3. **Institutional buyers** running tenders for rescue, patrol and training craft who need documented QC, ISO 2859-1 sampling plans and third-party inspection acceptance built into supply.

## Why meet the factory at a show

Every specification decision — hull material, tube fabric, console type, certification route — is cheaper to change on a stand than after tooling starts. Sitting down with the engineers who weld, test and ship the boats means MOQ tiers, lead times and CE scope get settled in one conversation instead of five email rounds.

## Book your slot

Meeting requests are handled in order of arrival. Write to **info@neptunor.com** with your program outline and preferred day, or submit the form via <a href="/oem-manufacturing">our OEM/ODM page</a> and we will confirm a time slot and stand details. Walk-ins are welcome too — but pre-booked meetings get the engineer, not just the sales team.

See you in Amsterdam this November.

The Air Deck floor is a high-pressure inflatable system used across Neptunor inflatable boat platforms. It replaces traditional plywood or slatted floors, cutting overall weight and reducing setup time to minutes.

Constructed from drop-stitch panels that inflate to high working pressure, the air deck forms a flat, rigid surface for standing, casting and moving about the boat while keeping the whole hull deflatable into a compact transport package.

## Specs you can quote

Air deck panels use the same high-tenacity fabric system as our tubes, with an anti-slip embossed surface and segmented folding layout so the floor stows inside the boat bag. Panels are validated with the same air-retention discipline applied to our tube chambers before a batch ships.
